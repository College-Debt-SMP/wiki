# College Debt lore

Starting at the beginning, I'm always interested in being able to play Minecraft with someone that I know of. That, unfortunately, was quite challenging back then when we were all introverts... until high school graduation day. My first ever friend group, the _Sheldon Bois_, was formulated after that day due to some crazy level of coincidence and prior acquaintances that would otherwise end up very differently. In hope that I could get everyone together in someway to test the water out on our collective relationships, I created my Minecraft server. When I first demonstrate this to a friend, they suggested calling it "Student Debt SMP" — since we were all in college at that point, the current namesake was created.

The Minecraft server itself and its underlying host starts out simple, but is then followed by quite a bunch of learning. It gave me the first exposure into using the Linux command line as well as container runtime such as Docker (if you don't know what these means, don't worry). Nothing was perfect even back then, as I did leave the server wide open for griefers from [2b2t](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2b2t) to join and ruined everything only a month later...

Beyond Minecraft, we did gradually span out to more multiplayer games like Among Us and Counter Strike, but for most of the time it's Minecraft since we're all too poor to get everyone to buy a certain game out of pocket :p.

There's no drama at all. All of us just want to chill.
