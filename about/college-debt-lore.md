# College Debt lore

The College Debt SMP is a private Minecraft survival multiplayer server created by CherryQuartzio and Zeroaf in 2023 for their friends from high school (and subsequently beyond that too). Just like any other Minecraft SMP, it serves as not only a Minecraft server to beat the game together, but also as a digital world that we can all vibe in.

There's no drama at all on the server. All of us are all good friends :)
