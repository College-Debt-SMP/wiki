# Cloud Debt 365

As a member of the SMP, you will get your own Cloud Debt 365 account with its own email address. The account exist mainly for communication and authentication purposes in the context of the College Debt SMP.

## Microsoft 365 Copilot addon

Upon request, your account is eligible to get complementary access to Microsoft 365 features. These include:

* Full access to Microsoft Office suite for Windows and macOS
* 1TB of OneDrive cloud storage
* Microsoft Team
