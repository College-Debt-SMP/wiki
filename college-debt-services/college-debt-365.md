# College Debt 365

As a member of the SMP, you will get your own Microsoft account to our organization with its own email address. The account exist mainly for communication and authentication purposes.

## Microsoft 365 Subscription

If you wished and have not gotten already, your account is eligible to get Microsoft 365 Copilot specific features at no additional charge. These include:

* Full access to Microsoft Office suite
* 1TB of OneDrive (for Business) cloud storage
* Microsoft Team

Contact the server administrator if you're in need to acquire one.
