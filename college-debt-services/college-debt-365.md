# College Debt 365

As a member of the SMP, you will get your own Microsoft account to our organization with its own domain for email. The account is mainly for communication and authentication purposes, but you're also eligible for Microsoft 365 Copilot benefits upon request, which include full access to Office and 1 TB of OneDrive storage.

Microsoft Teams is also available, but most member use Discord to chat.
