## Player & mob head

Killing a player will drop their head with the name of the killer attached in the description. Mobs also have a chance to drop their head upon killing.

## "Micro" Block

Using the Stonecutter with any blocks allow you to obtain the smaller variation of itself. They behave like player heads, but they're not actually head (well technically they are).
