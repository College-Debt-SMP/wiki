# Cloud Debt 365

As a member of the College Debt SMP, you are granted your own Cloud Debt 365 account with a unique email address that you can use to send and receive emails with. You will need it to access any other services on the Server Cluster.

## Security practices

We highly recommended that you enable two-factor authentication using a TOTP app. If possible, also have a password manager generate the account password for you. Having a physical security key is even better.

If you forget your password, contact one of the admins, and they will give you a temporary password to reset.

## Additional Benefits

Unless otherwise stated, your account comes with features equivalent to a [Microsoft 365 Business Standard](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-business-standard) subscription without the Copilot add-on. You may use them at your own will.

### Microsoft Edge Default Settings

When you sign into the Edge browser with your account, it should disable some of the settings deemed non-essential out of the box to provide a cleaner browsing experience with uBlock Origin bundled.
