# Free stuff

### Free Media Heck Yeah

This [website](https://fmhy.net/) provides a lot of resources for free digital content. For example:

* If you want to stream stuff in higher resolution on Discord without Nitro, then you can use the FakeNitro plugin on Vencord.
* If you want to sails the sea for any media, you got some good hints on there.
* Want to know some free alternatives to paid software? That site also got you covered.

I neither encouraged nor against piracy (please leave me alone FBI), but the best vibe comes from your own desire.

### Video games

Occasionally, there are PC games that [goes for free](https://freetokeep.gg/) within a limited time window for you to claim to keep. There's a dedicated announcement channel on the Discord server that sent out an alert if anything new came up

### For College Students

Many companies offer students [free or discounted access to paid services](https://www.buildincollege.com/dashboard) either as a limited trial time or until you finish your study at your school. You usually need to apply with your institution’s email and may have to submit a proof of enrollment.

### Banking and credit bonuses

Sometimes, banking/financial companies would do a promotion to entice you to do business with them, which usually comes in sign-up bonuses for a new account or credit card. Sites like [Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/) can give you an up to date list of on going promotions in addition to credit card reviews.

### State government benefits

Your state government do have programs like food stamp, unemployment benefit, and health insurance that you can apply for if you qualify.

* [California](https://benefitscal.com/)
* [Washington](https://www.dshs.wa.gov/)
